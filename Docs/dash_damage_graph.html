    Reading the wires

    Solid light wires are exec: they decide what runs next. Coloured dashed wires are data: they only feed values. Pure nodes have no exec pins — wire their output straight into the consumer.

    Hover a node to isolate its wires. Drag the canvas to pan, wheel to zoom, or press Fit.
